Card Of the Day

One tarot card a day, a question to sit with, and a journal of what it surfaced.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: write to bstrd.live@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

  • Can I draw again? No: one card a day, the same all day. That's the ritual. A new card is ready after midnight, your local time.
  • What is a 3-card spread? An optional deeper look: three cards for past, present and what could come next. You can draw one a day after a short ad.
  • Do I need an account? No. Sign in with your email (we send a 6-digit code) only if you want your journal kept safe and on all your devices.
  • I don't get reminders. Turn them on in Settings → Reminders and allow notifications for Card Of the Day in your phone's Settings.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Your account → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and removes your journal from our servers. Without the app, see Delete your account.
  • Is this fortune telling? No. The cards are prompts for reflection, not predictions.

Privacy Policy

Card Of the Day · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Card Of the Day ("the app", "we") gives you one tarot card a day as a prompt for reflection, and keeps a personal journal of your cards and notes. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. The app is operated by kBSTRD, Thailand. Contact: bstrd.live@gmail.com.

Short version

  • You can use the app without an account. Then your cards and notes stay on your phone and we store nothing about you on our servers.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address and your journal (the cards you drew, your 3-card spreads and your notes) so it's kept safe and appears on all your devices.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads (see below).
  • We don't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We don't sell your data.

What we collect and why

  • Email address: when you sign in with a one-time email code. Used to create your account and send you sign-in codes. Stored with Supabase (United States).
  • Your journal: when you're signed in, the card you drew each day (and whether it was reversed), your 3-card spreads, your notes and when they were written. Used only to show your journal to you and keep it in sync across your devices. Stored with Supabase (United States). We don't read your notes or use them for anything else.
  • Basic request data (IP address, time, device type): when the app syncs with your account. Used to run and protect the service; kept in Supabase's logs for a limited time.

On your device only (never sent to us): your journal when you're not signed in, your sign-in session, your reminder settings and the reminders themselves, and when ads were last shown (so they aren't shown too often). These are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads, a full-screen ad when the app opens (never before you've turned the day's card), and an optional rewarded ad you can watch to unlock a 3-card spread. These ads come from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: sign-in and database
  • Google AdMob: advertising (as described above)
  • Google (Gmail): sending sign-in codes by email

How long we keep data

Your email address and journal are kept until you delete your account. Logs are kept for a limited period by our providers.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by writing to bstrd.live@gmail.com. To delete your account and everything linked to it, open Settings → Your account → Delete account; it takes effect immediately. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, California's CCPA or Thailand's PDPA) you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

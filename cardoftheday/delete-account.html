Delete your account

Card Of the Day: Tarot Journal · by kBSTRD

An account is optional in Card Of the Day. If you signed in with your email, you can delete the account and everything stored with it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Card Of the Day and tap the gear icon on Today to open Settings.
  2. Tap Your account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately, and the app clears its journal from your phone.

Without the app

Email bstrd.live@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Card Of the Day account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and account
  • Your journal: the cards you drew, your 3-card spreads and all your notes

Nothing is kept afterwards, except short-lived service logs (such as IP address and time of a request) that our providers delete on their usual schedule, within 30 days. If you used the app without signing in, nothing about you is stored on our servers; deleting the app removes everything.
